# House voice: how to write and direct VO lines

Every line is generated on its own with `instruct = base_instruct + " " + direction`
(see `voice.json`). These rules and examples come from the Attendee App launch film, which the team liked.

## Rules

- **One short line per generation.** 3–14 words. Long sentences go flat.
- **Write to the picture.** Each line matches one beat or one on-screen action. Use spoken transitions
  between chapters ("Now, for your sponsors.") so it never sounds like one sentence per slide.
- **Professional and warm. No jokes or gags.** Personality comes from rhythm, contrast and confident,
  benefit-led wording, not humour.
- **Dynamics:** louder and brighter on payoffs, softer and slower on setup and the close.
- **Never claim what the product doesn't do.** If it isn't on screen in real UI, it isn't said as a feature.
- **Say the product as "Events'" plus the feature name**, e.g. "Events' Attendee App", "Events' coupon codes".
- **Lists:** one word per line with its own direction (see "Lists" below), or one line read evenly.
- **Playful "Aaand…" reveals: at most once per video**, and only for a real "one more thing" moment.

## Direction library (real lines from the film)

### Opening / setup
| Line | Direction |
|---|---|
| Then it got lost... in the inbox. | Low and wry, a small sigh before 'in the inbox', but clearly audible to the last word. |
| No branded app yet? | A light, friendly question, clear and well projected. |
| (any opening line) | Soft and reflective, a little slower and quieter, like opening a story. |

### Introducing the feature
| Line | Direction |
|---|---|
| Meet the Events' Attendee App, built right into the platform you already use. | Bright and proud, energy lifting, a smile in the voice; lean on 'Events' Attendee App'. |
| It already powered the attendee experience at HighLevel's own LevelUp event. | Proud and warm: a confident, understated brag, a smile in the voice, a little weight on 'LevelUp'. |

### Chapter turns
| Line | Direction |
|---|---|
| Now, for your sponsors. | A fresh, inviting turn to a new topic. |
| Now, for your sponsors. Group them into tiers, each linked to an external URL or an exhibitor. | Fresh energy for a new topic, crisp and clear. |
| Got advertisers? Place their ads on exactly the pages you choose. | An inviting, upbeat question with a smile, then confident and clear. |

### Explaining how it works
| Line | Direction |
|---|---|
| Group them into tiers, each linked to an external URL or an exhibitor. | Crisp, clear and confident. |
| Ads are separate. Place them on exactly the pages you choose. | Clear contrast on 'separate', then confident. |
| Staying in touch is automatic. Reminders and schedule changes reach attendees, speakers, and exhibitors. | Clear and steady, reassuring; a slight lift on 'automatic'. Say 'attendees, speakers, and exhibitors' as one smooth, flowing list. |
| Then shape the app around your event. Reorder and rename any section. | Light and creative. |
| Set up the Events' Attendee App once, | Confident and clear, building toward the payoff. |
| and reuse it for every event. | Satisfied and warm, relaxed and a little slower, with a contented smile. |

### Payoffs
| Line | Direction |
|---|---|
| Every connection becomes a lead for the exhibitor, ready to export in one click. | A confident payoff with weight on 'lead', then a light, satisfied 'ready to export'. |
| And every view, and every click, is tracked. | Deliberate and confident, a little emphasis on each 'every'. |
| Add custom sections too, like an interactive venue map, so attendees find Booth 21 in seconds. | Enthusiastic, a small smile on 'Booth 21'. |
| Our team will build it for you. | Warm, calm and reassuring, a little slower, like a sincere promise; gentle weight on 'build it for you'. |

### Lists (one word per generation)
| Line | Direction |
|---|---|
| Home. | One crisp word, neutral, starting a list. |
| Agenda. | One crisp word, a little more energy, continuing a list. |
| Speakers. | One crisp word, rising energy in a list. |
| Exhibitors. | One crisp word, the emphatic end of a list. |
| Home. Agenda. Speakers. Exhibitors. | Read the list crisply and evenly: steady energy on every word, none louder or softer than the others. |

### Close (end card)
| Line | Direction |
|---|---|
| The Events' Attendee App, from HighLevel. In every pocket. | A grand, warm close: measured, a pause between phrases, proud. |
| Available now. | Confident and warm, a satisfied smile, natural speaking volume; clear and assured, never shouted. |
