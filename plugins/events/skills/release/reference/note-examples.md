Overview:
Registration is just the beginning. The real value of an event is everything that happens after.
Events' Attendee App brings discovery, tickets, schedules, networking, exhibitors, sponsors and live updates together in HighLevel's Client Portal, on the web, iOS and Android, and inside your own branded app. It's now rolling out.

Your members already log in to your Client Portal for courses and communities. Now your events live there too.

And here's the exciting part: Events' Attendee App also powered the attendee experience at HighLevel's LevelUp event! :boom:





:sparkles: What's New?

:compass: Events in Your Client Portal

Events now has its own home in the Client Portal. Members see what's live right now, browse upcoming and past events, filter by location, and buy tickets in a few clicks: choose ticket types and quantities, apply a coupon, and check out.
You choose what's listed. Turn on "Make this event discoverable" to show an event to all portal members, or keep it visible only to the people taking part.



:admission_tickets: From Checkout Straight Into the Event

Attendees go from checkout into the event with no separate password. Every ticket lives in one place, with its QR code, venue directions and a one-click download.
On mobile, they can save tickets to Apple Wallet with one tap.
People already registered get access automatically when you turn the app on.



:handshake: Connections That Outlast the Event

Find attendees, speakers and exhibitors, send a personal connection request or scan their QR badge, and keep talking in chat. Connections carry across every event you host, and members choose whether they appear in the attendee list.



:date: A Personal Agenda for Every Attendee

Browse sessions by day, search, and save the ones that matter to My schedule. Get to know the speakers before you arrive.
Organizers can limit sessions to specific ticket types, such as VIP-only experiences, and see how many people saved each session.



:briefcase: Booth Visits That Become Leads

Attendees browse exhibitors by tier, bookmark the booths worth visiting, and connect with booth teams. Exhibitors turn those connections and badge scans into leads, manage them in the Exhibitor Center, and export them.



:mega: Turn Your Event Into Ad Space

Sell ad placements to your sponsors right inside the event. Feature a full ad on the event home, run banner ads on the pages you choose, and rotate several ads in the same spot. Target each ad to everyone, to event members, or to specific roles, and link it to the sponsor's site.



:star2: Showcase Your Sponsors

Show sponsors in tiers on the event page, so every partner gets the visibility they paid for.



:bell: The Right Message, Right on Time

Keep attendees, speakers and exhibitors informed with automated confirmations and reminders by email, SMS, in-app notifications and push. Send announcements now or schedule them, aimed at the roles you pick. Every message opens the right part of the event.



:art: Make It Yours

Rename, reorder or hide sections, and add your own, such as venue maps, PDFs, embeds and links. Deliver it all in the Client Portal on the web and on mobile, or in your own branded app.





:bulb: Why This Matters

• For attendees: everything they need before, during and after the event, in one place and on any device.
• For organizers: more ticket sales from members you already have, fewer emails, and a clear picture of what interests people.
• For exhibitors and sponsors: visibility in front of the right people, and leads they can follow up on.





:rocket: How to Get Started

1. Go to Events → select an event → Attendee App.
2. Turn on "Attendee app access". If prompted, click "Upgrade Client Portal" first. It takes about a minute.
3. Turn on "Make this event discoverable" to list it for all portal members.

Attendees sign in on the web, in the HighLevel Client Portal app, or in your branded app.
One platform. Every attendee. A more connected event experience.

:zap: `NEW` Trigger: Event Registration
Every event registration can now kick off an automated journey!

* Send instant confirmation emails and SMS.
* Schedule pre-event reminders and follow-ups.
* Automatically tag and segment attendees.
* Notify your team or create sales opportunities.



:admission_tickets: `NEW` Action: Register for Ticketed Event
Automatically register contacts for ticketed events through workflows.

* Convert leads from Facebook Ads, funnels, and forms into event registrants.
* Register qualified prospects for webinars, workshops, or exclusive events.
* Connect your existing marketing campaigns directly to event registrations.



:white_tick: `NEW` Action: Register for RSVP Event
Automatically RSVP contacts for events using workflows.

* RSVP leads captured through forms and landing pages.
* Register customers for onboarding sessions or webinars.
* Build automated invitation and registration journeys.







:bulb: Automations You Can Build

* Facebook Lead Ad → Event Registration → Confirmation Email
* Form Submission → RSVP Event → Reminder Sequence
* Event Registration → Add Tag → Send SMS → Follow-up Campaign
* Event Registration → Create Opportunity → Notify Sales Team









:rocket: Why It Matters

* No more manual registrations: Turn your existing lead sources into event registration channels.
* Engage attendees instantly: Automate confirmations, reminders, and personalized follow-ups.
* Connect your entire funnel: Bring marketing, CRM, events, and automation together.
* Scale effortlessly: From a handful of attendees to thousands, build repeatable automated event journeys.
